# Adventure Works Product and Territory Analysis

## Introduction
This report provides an in-depth analysis of product and territory performance within the Adventure Works dataset. It evaluates key performance metrics, sales trends, and the influence of external factors. The goal is to offer actionable insights to drive better decision-making for product development, market strategy, and resource allocation across various territories.
## Repository Overview
In this repository, you will find the following:
- **README.md**: Provides an overview of the project and its objectives.
- **Week4Product_And_Territory_Analysis.ipynb**: A Jupyter Notebook containing Python code for connecting to the database, executing SQL queries, and performing exploratory data analysis.
## Objectives
 The objective of this project is to examine how different products perform over time, including their sales patterns, lifecycle stages (e.g., introduction, growth, maturity, and decline),
 and how sales are distributed across different channels or customer segments.
## Sales Performance Analysis Report
### 1. Product Analysis Report: Performance Metrics and Trends
- **Summary Visualizations**: We have created visualizations to summarize Adventure Works' product sales and costs, providing insights into the company's profitability and overall performance across all products.
- **Key Metrics**: The visuals highlight key performance metrics, trends, and month-to-month sales distribution, including total sales, total units sold, profit figures, and a ranking of the top 10 best-selling products.

- **External Factors Analysis**: We have created visualizations to analyze product and territory performance, including the impact of external factors such as economic conditions, marketing campaigns, and seasonal effects.
## Conclusion
The aim of this analysis is to derive actionable insights that can drive better product decisions and improve resource allocation across territories. By understanding product performance and territory dynamics, we can enhance overall strategic planning and operational efficiency.
